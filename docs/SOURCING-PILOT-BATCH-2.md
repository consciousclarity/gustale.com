# Sourcing pilot — Batch 2: tamarind, coconut milk, cassava

**Researched:** 2026-11-11 · **Stage A** (a research document, for the
owner's review — nothing is published)

**Batch 2 = 3 ingredients × 4 countries (DE, ID, CH, US) = 12 pairs.**
Batch 1 covered curry leaves, gochujang and fish sauce in DE and ID
only, so this batch adds Switzerland and the United States, and three
ingredients that behave differently: tamarind is a **mixed** market in
Indonesia, coconut milk has a **local** and an **imported** strand,
and cassava is **wholly local**.

## 2. The import dependency, from the registry

**Indonesia's import dependency is settled by the food regulator's own
registration, not by a brand name.** A product registered `MD` with
**no importer** is made by an Indonesian company; `ML` with an importer
ID is made abroad.

**The registry's search matches cosmetics as well as food.** The
`tamarind` query returns body scrubs and perfumes; only the
`MD`/`ML` registrations are food. **Every count below filters to
`MD`/`ML` first.**

### 2.1 Tamarind — a MIXED market

| | registrations |
|---|---|
| `MD` (made in Indonesia, no importer) | **14** |
| `ML` (imported, with an importer) | **15** |

The two plain tamarind registrations are **both imported**:

| registration | product | importer |
|---|---|---|
| `ML 255629005669` | Asam Jawa Tamarind, 200 g / 500 g / 1 kg / 5 kg | `T6917` |
| `ML 010971001701754` | Pasta Asam Jawa (Tamarind Paste), 227 g glass jar | `P127025` |

But the **MD** strand is real and it is food, not cosmetics — "SIRUP
RASA ASAM JAWA (Tamarind Syrup)" `MD 267231127051` by PT. Kiantaka
Rasa, "MANISAN ASEM (Preserved Tamarind)" `MD 040927000900296` by
SUSAN PANGAN SEHAT, and 12 more.

**So the country row says: mixed.** Some tamarind on the Indonesian
shelf is imported and some is made in Indonesia, and **the
availability is common** because the *product* is common, regardless
of which strand a given brand is on.

## 5. What I could not source, and why

**Held back from the load, not silently dropped:**

1. **US tamarind — no ordinary-retail product page.** The Spice House
   search for "tamarind" reports "5 results found", but **none of the
   five is tamarind**: the result grid holds Lakeshore Shallot & Herb
   $9.99, Green Chimichurri Mix $9.99, Great North Maple Garlic $9.99
   and Tuscan Harvest $9.49. Those are the *trending* products, and
   **their prices do not belong to tamarind**. Whole Foods, Trader
   Joe's, H Mart, Modern Market and grocery.com all returned no
   product-level price for tamarind. **No US tamarind price is
   asserted.**
2. **US cassava — no product page.** The US searches for cassava were
   not reached before the research budget ran out.
3. **The cassava substitutes (cassava flour, tapioca starch) — not
   researched** in DE, CH or US.
4. **The German fresh cassava and fresh coconut milk** — not stocked
   in the German pages fetched; only the dried and the canned forms
   were found.
5. **The Indonesian tamarind paste price** — the plain paste
   registration exists in the registry (`ML 010971001701754`, 227 g),
   but no *retail* price for a paste product was found. **Not
   established.**
6. **A German tamarind `dried` form and a Swiss cassava `dried` form**
   — not found in the pages fetched.

**Why the US is thin.** The US sites are the hardest of the four:
Whole Foods and Trader Joe's render their catalogues client-side and
the fetched pages carried no prices, and H Mart's and Modern Market's
search pages returned no product rows. **A page that shows no price
is not evidence that the product is unavailable**, and none of these
rows asserts that.

## 6. How this document was checked

- **Every cited URL was fetched.** The prices were read from the
  **target product's own price block on its own page** — Migros's
  "2 Franken 90" format, Asia Shop's "2,49€inkl. MwSt. + Grundpreis"
  format, Tokopedia's "Subtotal Rp…" block.
- **The related-product trap was caught.** The Spice House "tamarind"
  search returned four unrelated trending products with their prices.
  Those prices are **not** in the table, and the US tamarind row is
  held back rather than filled with them.
- **Search-page ranges are marked as ranges**, and are never presented
  as a product's price.
- **Stock state is recorded where the page states it**: two of the
  three German tamarind pages say "Nicht vorrätig".
- **The import dependency comes from the registry**, and every count
  filters to the `MD`/`ML` food registrations first.
- **The research was done by one researcher** and the figures were
  re-read from the fetched pages afterwards, by the second pass. The
  **US** rows are the ones that did not survive that process intact:
  they are held back.
