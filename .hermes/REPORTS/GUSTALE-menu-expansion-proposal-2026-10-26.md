## 1. A correction to the task brief, before the dishes

The task brief asks for "menu/concept document", "price tier", "format (casual/fine/fusion)" and "starters/mains" sections. **Gustale has none of these** — and the reason matters, because it changes what the deliverable should be.

`PROJECT.md` line 7 states the product:

> *"gustale.com — open culinary atlas / food encyclopedia: dishes, regions (geography), families/lineages, sources, media, contributor moderation."*

`ROADMAP.md` §7 gives the objective:

> *"Building the World's Most Credible and Interactive Encyclopedia of Culinary Heritage"*

There is no menu, no price tier, and no service format, because **Gustale is not a restaurant — it is a reference work.** The live site's top-level navigation is `/regions`, `/families`, `/lineages`, `/about` — geographic and taxonomic browsing, not ordering.

**What this means for the proposal:**

| brief asked for | this proposal delivers instead |
|---|---|
| menu sections (starters/mains) | **encyclopedia article sections** (ROADMAP §8: Introduction · At a glance · Historical background · Cultural significance · Where it is found · Regional variations · Ingredients · Preparation · Historical journey · Culinary relatives · Sources · Recipes · Community knowledge · Editorial history) |
| price tier | **not applicable** — there are no prices on Gustale |
| "fits the concept" | fit is judged against the **encyclopedia mission and its credibility bar**, not a service format |
| "unexpected" | means **not a dish a reader would predict**, while still being a real, traceable tradition |

I have kept every other instruction intact: 8–12 dishes, name, origin (country + specific region), one-line description, why it fits, why it is unexpected, grouped by section, sourcing difficulty flagged, no invented fusion gimmicks, no duplicates.

**Two operational constraints from `.hermes/RULES.md` that this proposal respects:**

> *"Never invent citations; Wikipedia is never the sole source for a contested claim."* (line 27)

> *"No bulk content generation before phase gate 1 → 2."* (`ROADMAP_EXECUTION.md` §1)

So: every dish below is a **real, verifiable traditional dish** with a named Wikipedia article (the discovery source), but **none of them is ready to publish** — each needs a second, non-Wikipedia source before it goes live, and the bulk addition is gated behind GUS-014 + GUS-026 + GUS-028. This document is a **proposal**, exactly as briefed.

### Section: Introduction / At a glance — dishes that anchor a new region

The four Georgian dishes are the strongest single addition: the Caucasus is entirely absent, Georgian cuisine has its own distinct food culture (wine, cheese, smoke), and it sits geographically between the Turkish and Levantine entries already in the catalog.

| # | Dish | Origin (country → region) | What it is | Why it fits Gustale | Why it is unexpected | Primary section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Khinkali** | Georgia → Kakheti (mountainous eastern Georgia) | A dumpling of twisted dough knobs filled with spiced meat, fish or vegetables, eaten by the piece. | The catalog has 6 dumplings (gyoza, jiaozi, momo, pierogi, xiaolongbao, manti) and no Georgian cuisine at all — this is the missing member of a family that already exists. | Dumplings are globally recognisable; a Georgian dumpling with a filling you eat with your hands and by the piece is not what readers picture when they hear "dumpling". | At a glance · Ingredients |
| 2 | **Khachapuri** | Georgia → Adjara (southwestern Georgia) | Leavened bread with a pocket of cheese and egg in the centre, baked on the open hearth. | ROADMAP §8 makes "Regional variations" and "Culinary relatives" first-class sections. Khachapuri is the single best anchor for a Georgian article because the *Adjarian* boat-shaped form and the *Imeretian* double-baked form are a documented regional variation pair — the exact relationship the encyclopedia is built to show. | "Cheese bread" is a common mental image; a boat-shaped bread with molten cheese and a raw egg, torn open at the table, is not. | Regional variations · Culinary relatives |
| 3 | **Nigvziani badrijani** | Georgia → Adjara | Fried eggplant filled with spiced walnut and garlic paste, topped with pomegranate seeds. | Gives the Georgian article a *vegetarian* entry and a second Adjarian dish, and introduces walnut as a savoury ingredient. | It looks like an ordinary fried aubergine; the walnut-and-garlic filling is the surprise, and Adjara is not a place most readers can place. | Ingredients · Cultural significance |
| 4 | **Lobio** | Georgia → Samegrelo (western Georgia) | A dish of prepared beans — cooked or stewed — with coriander, walnuts, garlic and onion; served hot or cold, with many local forms. | A bean dish gives the catalog a *staple/communal* entry, which is a real gap: the current catalog is almost entirely composed or plated food. | "Beans" is not a dish anyone expects to find in a world-cuisine encyclopedia; the Georgian version is an entire category with a dozen named regional variants. | Cultural significance · Regional variations |

### Section: Ingredients / Preparation — the West African and Central Asian gap

| # | Dish | Origin (country → region) | What it is | Why it fits Gustale | Why it is unexpected | Primary section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Attiéké** | Côte d'Ivoire → Abidjan (and across the coast) | Fermented cassava pulp, grated or granulated into couscous-like grains and steamed, eaten with a rich sauce. | A *fermented staple* — the catalog has `fermented-dish` as a family with **zero** dishes in it. | 7 | **Suya** | Nigeria → Kano (Hausa) | Thin-sliced spiced beef, lamb or goat on wooden skewers, smoke-grilled over charcoal, with onion and chili. | `kebab` already exists with 8 dishes, all from the Mediterranean and Middle East (anticuchos, satay, sate-lilit, yakitori, doner-kebab, iskender-kebab, kofta, babi-guling). ## 5. Sourcing difficulty

**All 12 are composed dishes** — no game, no endangered species, no imported specialty. Nothing here needs a specialist importer.

| Dish | Hardest ingredient | Difficulty | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Waakye** | dried sorghum leaves, powdered limestone | 🟡 **medium** | Both are ordinary West African items, but "dried sorghum leaf" (hammayero) and edible limestone (kaun) are unlikely in a general grocery. The leaves are the flavouring and could be substituted with bay; the limestone is the interesting part and is the honest reason to source it. |
| **Attiéké** | fermented cassava pulp | 🟡 **medium** | Cassava is globally available, but the *fermented* pulp is not a supermarket item. Fermenting at home is a multi-day process. |
| **Suya** | skewers, charcoal, spiced groundnut (yaji) | 🟢 **easy** | Beef, onion, chili, peanut and wood are all obtainable. The yaji blend is peanuts + ginger + garlic + cayenne + grains of selenium — locally made, not imported. |
| **Beshbarmak** | horsemeat (optional), large sheep/lamb | 🟢 **easy** | Lamb is widely available. The horsemeat is a traditional optional component, not required. The noodles are bought or hand-rolled. |
| **Khinkali** | frozen/dried dumpling wrappers or hand-rolled dough | 🟢 **easy** | Standard dough. Filling is spiced meat. |
| **Khachapuri** | suluguni or imeruli cheese, egg | 🟢 **easy** | Sulugini is a real cheese, but a blend of kefalotyri + halloumi works, and feta is a defensible substitute. |
| **Nigvziani badrijani** | walnuts, garlic, pomegranate seeds | 🟢 **easy** | All globally available. |
| **Lobio** | beans, walnuts, garlic, coriander | 🟢 **easy** | Completely ordinary. |
| **Samsa** | lamb fat, onion, pastry dough | 🟡 **medium** | The lamb fat is the traditional richness and is what makes a samsa a samsa; lean mince works but is a different dish. |
| **Laghman** | hand-pulled noodles | 🟡 **medium** | The noodles should be hand-pulled, which is a technique, not a product — this is a *process* sourcing note rather than an ingredient one. |
| **Bolinhos de bacalhau** | salt cod | 🟡 **medium** | Salt cod (bacalhau) is the defining ingredient and is widely sold preserved. Fresh is not needed. |
| **Tortilla de patatas** | eggs, potatoes, neutral oil | 🟢 **easy** | The most available dish on this list. |

**Summary:** 7 easy, 5 medium. **None is a blocker.**

## 6. Things Alejandro should decide before anything is created

These are not objections to the list — they are decisions I should not make unilaterally.

**a) `bolinhos de bacalhau` vs the existing `pastéis de bacalhau`.** Both are salt-cod and potato. The catalog already has `pastéis de bacalhau`. Are these one dish with two names (in which case it is a **regional variation** record, not a new dish), or two genuinely distinct dishes? They are distinct — one is a custard tart, the other a fried cake — but they share the name. **My recommendation: add it as a distinct dish and link the two with a `dish_relations` row, so the encyclopedia shows the relationship rather than silently duplicating it.**

**b) The `filled-pastry` family now has 2 members and this proposal adds a third.** `samsa` is a *baked* pastry parcel; `empanada` and `samosa` are *fried*. The taxonomy's dumpling/filled-pastry line is **boiled-or-steamed vs baked-or-fried** — so a baked samsa is a filled-pastry on the current rule. **No conflict**, but the family will have 3 members. Worth confirming the rule reads as intended for baked goods.

**c) `attieke` needs a family that does not exist yet.** The catalog's families have no cassava-root or "fermented grain" family. `fermented-dish` exists and is currently **empty**, so attiéké fits there — but "fermented cassava" as a family would be more precise. **My recommendation: use `fermented-dish` (it exists, it is empty, and the taxonomy should not sprout a one-member family).**

**d) `lobio` fits `stew` or `soup`, but it is a *bean dish*.** The catalog's `stew` has 11 members, mostly meat and legume stews. **My recommendation: `stew`**, with the bean character carried by `ingredient_category`.

**e) `waakye` is a `rice-dish` and also a breakfast.** The catalog has `breakfast` as a course value with **zero** dishes. Waakye would be its first. **This is a real find** — the breakfast course exists in the vocabulary and is entirely empty.

**f) `suya` is `kebab` — but the existing kebab members are mostly minced or satay-style.** Suya is *sliced*, not minced. The `kebab` family does not currently distinguish. **Not a blocker** (the family is broad), but a note for whoever writes the encyclopedia entry.

**g) Coverage check on the 19 dishes with no cuisine set.** Separately from this proposal, **19 of the 121 published dishes have no `dish_cuisine`** (acaraje, borscht, couscous, goulash-hungarian, hamburger-american, jollof-rice, khao-soi, manto, moussaka-greek, poutine, tagine-moroccan, tostones, vindaloo, wiener-schnitzel, and others). That is a **data gap in the existing catalog**, not a gap in this proposal — and it is larger than most of what this proposal addresses. Worth a separate backlog item.

## 7. What happens next (if approved)

**Nothing is created by this document.** On approval, the work would be:

1. **Re-source all 12 against a second, non-Wikipedia source** — GUS-026's rule is that Wikipedia is never the sole basis for a contested claim, and these are all "documented" claims, so each needs at least one S1–S5 source. **GUS-026 is still `in_progress`**, so this cannot start until it is decided.
2. **Research and write** each dish's encyclopedia article to the ROADMAP §8 structure.
3. **Create the dishes** with their 7 dimensions, following the ADR-001 taxonomy now in the live database.
4. **Add images** under `.hermes/IMAGE_STYLE.md` (documentary field photography, authentic serveware, no Western china).
5. **Create the records** through the Gustale API — not by direct database writes.

**The phase gate applies.** `ROADMAP_EXECUTION.md` §1: *"No bulk content generation (new dishes, mass rewrites of descriptions) before gate 1 → 2."* Gate 1→2 requires **GUS-014 + GUS-026 + GUS-028** live on the three reference dishes. GUS-014 is now implemented; **GUS-026 and GUS-028 are not**. So these 12 cannot be created until those land — which is the correct order anyway, because without the claims model a new dish would be born with the same single-Wikipedia-citation weakness that GUS-011 found across all 121 existing dishes.

**My recommendation: accept the list, and hold creation until GUS-026 and GUS-028 are live.** The research can start now in parallel, since research is read-only.
